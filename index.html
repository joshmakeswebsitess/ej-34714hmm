About EZJournal

Your entries stay on this device

Everything you enter is saved in this browser, on this device only. It isn't sent anywhere, and nobody else can see it, including whoever shared this link with you. Your other devices, and other browsers on this device, each keep their own separate copy.

Always use the same link

Your browser ties your entries to this exact web address. If you open EZJournal from a different link, it starts out empty. Bookmark this one.

Your entries can be erased

There's no backup and no syncing. Clearing your browser's site data or history erases your entries, and private or incognito windows don't keep anything.

iPhone and iPad: open it at least every few days

Apple's browser engine, which every browser on iPhone and iPad uses (including Chrome), may erase a site's saved data after 7 days of browser use without opening that site. Opening EZJournal regularly prevents this. A Home Screen app (added with Safari's Share button) is supposed to be exempt, but it keeps its own copy of your data, separate from the browser. Use only one of the two on each device.

Space is limited

Browsers only give each site a few megabytes. Photos in your closet, recipes, and boards use it up fastest. If it fills up, EZJournal will tell you, and you'll need to remove something to add more.

Not medical advice

EZJournal is a personal tracker. The calorie, water, and workout tools are not medical, nutrition, or fitness advice. Talk to a professional about your health.

Provided as-is

This is a hobby project shared among friends, with no guarantees. Use it at your own risk.

Privacy

EZJournal doesn't collect, track, or send your entries anywhere, and it has no ads or analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, like most websites.

Please keep the link private
